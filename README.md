## 1. Quick Start

### Option A: Direct Double-Click (`file://` Mode)
This prototype is engineered with **zero build step and zero runtime external dependencies**.
Simply double-click `index.html` in File Explorer to run it instantly in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox. All scripts and styles are structured without CORS restrictions on local protocols.

### Option B: Local Web Server
```bash
# Python 3
python -m http.server 8080

# Or Node.js
npx serve .
```
Open `http://localhost:8080` in any desktop or mobile browser.

### Option C: Hosting on GitHub Pages
- Host on GitHub Pages from root (`main` branch `/` root).

## 3. The 8-Step Demo Script (60-Second Talk Tracks for Judges)

Use the built-in **Presenter Panel** (toggle with `P` or the floating FAB) and press `N` (Next) and `B` (Back) to advance through the guided tour.

### Step 1: Discover — "The Anti-Directory" (60s)
> *"Judges, when an Indian bride starts planning today, she faces chaos: hundreds of Instagram profiles, opaque 'DM for pricing' quotes, and intense anxiety that her artist will cancel on muhurat morning. Naturals does not need to build another directory. We build certainty through five ironclad promises: transparent pricing by scope, an objective trial in a standardized salon chair, a single coordinated team, a pre-briefed named backup within 20 minutes, and verified proof from finished weddings. Notice there is zero paywall or login required to view real prices."*

### Step 2: Build a Price — "Scope-Based Transparency" (60s)
> *"Here, the bride configures her wedding scope across Muhurat, Reception, Mehendi, and Haldi, selecting exact family guest counts. Watch how the rate card calculates transparently in real time. We don't quote arbitrary lump sums based on hotel stars or family affluence. Every service—hair, HD makeup, saree pleating, assistant touch-up kits—is itemised with 18% GST included. What you see is what you pay."*

### Step 3: Lock It — "Guaranteed in Writing" (60s)
> *"On this hero screen, the bride toggles 'Lock this price'. The number freezes, a LOCKED badge appears, and Naturals issues a written price guarantee. If the bride clicks 'Download scope as PDF', our clean print-engine generates a formal scope contract with explicit signoff lines. No morning-of renegotiations, no sudden travel charges, no awkward upselling at 5 AM."*

### Step 4: Trial & Shade Log — "The Certified Salon Chair" (60s)
> *"Competitors offer unstandardized home trials or digital consultations. Naturals leverages its physical 700+ salon footprint. The bride sits in a certified salon chair under calibrated 5000K daylight and flash photography tests. Her skin type, product sensitivities, and exact foundation code—such as `NAT-O3 Fair Warm`—are logged into the Naturals system. The ₹1,500 trial fee is credited in full when she confirms her wedding day."*

### Step 5: Team & Named Backup — "The Contingency Promise" (60s)
> *"Here is the team assigned to her wedding: Ananya as lead artist, Priya for hair, Ramya for saree pleating, and Sneha as her dedicated desk coordinator. But most importantly: look at Divya R., marked with the ARMED pill. She is a named standby artist just 3.8 km away, pre-synced with the bride's trial notes. Below is our written guarantee: 'Late 30 min: backup moves in. Cancels: cover in 2 hours, or refund plus credit.' Complete peace of mind."*

### Step 6: Wedding Day & Late Lead Incident — "The Failure Case" (60s)
> *"This is the emotional centerpiece of our product. On the wedding morning, our live timeline tracks operational check-ins. Now watch: I'll click 'Simulate: Lead is 30 min late'. The timeline turns red—a traffic blockade. In a freelance world, the bride enters panic. In Naturals Bridal, our central desk auto-dispatches Divya. An ETA countdown appears. Divya arrives with the exact shade code NAT-O3, the status returns to green, and the Muhurat look starts on schedule. We designed the failure case so the bride never experiences failure."*

### Step 7: Proof Wall — "Unvarnished Reality" (60s)
> *"Our Proof Wall does not permit paid endorsements or influencer puffery. Only brides with completed, paid bookings can post. They score us on three transparent metrics: 'Looked like me', 'Team on time', and 'Price matched the card'. Because every review is linked to an auditable contract, brides trust our proof implicitly."*

### Step 8: HQ Control Room & Pilot Governance — "Downside Protection" (60s)
> *"Switching to the Executive Control Room. We propose a 12-week pilot across 40 certified hubs and 300 artists in Chennai and Coimbatore, funded by ₹3.0 crore released in milestone tranches. Four quantitative Go/Stop gates govern capital deployment. If you drag the pilot progression slider, you can test how gates respond. If any metric enters the STOP zone—such as artist leakage exceeding 50%—the next tranche is automatically frozen. This protects Naturals' balance sheet while testing unit economics in the field."*

## 5. Keyboard Navigation & Accessibility

- `P`: Toggle Presenter Panel
- `N`: Next Tour Step
- `B`: Previous Tour Step
- `R`: Reset Demo to clean state
- `1`: Switch to Bride App
- `2`: Switch to Artist OS
- `3`: Switch to Hub Owner Dashboard
- `4`: Switch to Naturals HQ Control Room
- **Screen Reader Support**: `aria-live="polite"` announces all wedding day timeline advancements and incident dispatches.
- **Motion Reduction**: Respects OS `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
