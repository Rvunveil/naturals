# Naturals Bridal: Design Decisions & Strategic Mapping

> **BSmart x Naturals NxtGen Innovators Challenge 2026**  
> *Case Question: "How can Naturals dominate the bridal grooming market?"*

---

## 1. Strategic Rationale: The "Book Certainty" Thesis

The bridal grooming market in India is not constrained by a shortage of discovery platforms; it is fractured by a **deficit of certainty**. Brides navigate opaque Instagram DM pricing, fear day-of cancellations on peak Muhurat dates, and worry about unstandardised makeup shades.

Instead of building another directory, Naturals leverages its physical salon network (700+ salons) and Academy infrastructure to offer **operational certainty**.

---

## 2. Screen-to-Promise Strategic Matrix

| Screen | Core Promise Proved | Strategic Rationale & Case Alignment |

| **11. HQ Control Room** | Capital Preservation | 12-week pilot with ₹3.0 Cr released in milestone tranches. Governed by 4 quantitative Go/Stop gates to protect balance sheet. |

---

## 3. The 4 Quantitative Go/Stop Gates

1. **Gate 1: Trial Conversion (GO >= 15%, STOP < 8%)**: Proves willingness to pay for salon-chair certainty.
2. **Gate 2: Artist Leakage (GO <= 30%, STOP > 50%)**: Protects platform ecosystem from off-platform bypass.
3. **Gate 3: Peak-Date Cover (GO >= 90%, STOP < 80%)**: Ensures standby contingency holds during peak wedding muhurats.
4. **Gate 4: Hub New Bride Share (GO >= 60%, STOP < 40%)**: Validates salon footfall acquisition for franchise owners.
